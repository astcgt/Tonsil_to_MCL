Mantle cell lymphoma is pinned to one normal B-cell state, and uses it to open a ferroptosis-defence programme

套細胞淋巴瘤被釘在單一正常 B 細胞狀態，並藉此開啟一套抗鐵死亡程式

Yi Liu

github.com/astcgt/Tonsil_to_MCL · all primary data public · no bench experimentsgithub.com/astcgt/Tonsil_to_MCL · 全部為公開資料 · 未進行濕實驗

Massoni-Badosa et al. built an atlas of >556,000 human tonsil cells and, in its final result, used it as a reference for two mantle cell lymphomas (MCL). Reproducing it from their raw multiome data, we recover both genomic findings exactly. Their transcriptional conclusion does not survive quantification: matching each of 19,975 tumour cells to 46 normal B-cell states, 90.0% match non-class-switch memory B cells, not the activated-naive-to-GC-committed interval the paper names — and the match is more concentrated than in the normal B cells beside them (2.89 vs 3.32 effective states, p = 1.3 × 10−27). The tumour is not poised along a maturation axis; it is pinned — which is what makes a subtraction well posed. What survives it is not proliferation but one seven-letter site, TGACTCA, opening genome-wide, and a cystine-import → glutathione → ferroptosis-defence axis that matched normal cells never use. The tumour depends on five consecutive genes of that axis, carries no mutation in any, and the literature on it is empty. We design a 90-residue binder against MafG, the obligate partner at the site, passing all four wet-lab-calibrated filters.

Massoni-Badosa 等人建立了超過 556,000 個人類扁桃體細胞的圖譜，並在最後一項結果中將其用作兩例套細胞淋巴瘤（MCL）的參考座標。我們自其原始 multiome 資料重跑該分析，完整重現其兩項基因體層面的發現。但其轉錄層面的結論無法通過定量檢驗：將 19,975 個腫瘤細胞逐一對位至 46 個正常 B 細胞狀態後，90.0% 對應到非類別轉換記憶 B 細胞，而非論文所指的「活化初始 B 至 GC 承諾」區間；且其對位較鄰近的正常 B 細胞更為集中（有效狀態數 2.89 對 3.32，p = 1.3 × 10−27）。腫瘤並非沿成熟軸線游移，而是被釘住於單一狀態，相減因此得以定義良好。相減後殘餘的並非增殖訊號，而是一個七字母位點 TGACTCA 的全基因組開啟，以及一條配對正常細胞從不啟用的「胱胺酸輸入 → 穀胱甘肽 → 抗鐵死亡」軸線。腫瘤連續依賴該軸線五個基因，其上不帶任何突變，而相關文獻幾近空白。我們針對該位點的必要夥伴 MafG 設計出 90 殘基結合劑，通過全部四項以濕實驗校準的門檻。

1Revisiting the published analysis重現已發表的分析

The Human Tonsil Atlas is a reference, and its last result section demonstrates what a reference is for. Having annotated 121 cell types and states across 17 tonsils, the authors profiled two tonsillar conventional MCL samples by 10x Multiome (M102 and M413; ages 64 and 80, both male), kept the CD19+ fraction, discarded the accessibility data, and read the tumour transcriptomes "in the context of our tonsil atlas". Three claims followed (their Fig. 7G–I, M102 only), and from them the conclusion that "MCL cells are not frozen in a particular maturation stage, but they retain a certain differentiation potential of normal B cells."

人類扁桃體圖譜是一套參考座標，而其最後一節結果正是在示範參考座標的用途。作者在 17 例扁桃體上註釋 121 種細胞型別與狀態後，以 10x Multiome 檢測兩例扁桃體原發的典型 MCL（M102 與 M413；64 與 80 歲，皆男性），僅保留 CD19+ 部分、捨棄可及性資料，並將腫瘤轉錄體置於「扁桃體圖譜的脈絡」中解讀。由此得到三項主張（其 Fig. 7G–I，僅展示 M102），並據以得出結論：「MCL 細胞並未被凍結在某個特定成熟階段，而是保留了正常 B 細胞的某種分化潛能。」

We rebuilt the analysis from the same deposited object: 20,794 CD19+ cells, of which 19,975 are tumour and 819 residual normal B cells in the same samples (M102: 10,030 cells, only 29 normal; M413: 10,764 with 790). Each claim was re-tested with a stated statistic rather than by eye (Fig. 2).

我們自同一份公開物件重建分析：20,794 個 CD19+ 細胞，其中 19,975 個為腫瘤細胞、819 個為同檢體殘存的正常 B 細胞（M102 共 10,030 個，其中僅 29 個正常；M413 共 10,764 個，其中 790 個正常）。每項主張均以明確的統計量重新檢定，而非以肉眼判讀（圖 2）。

Why the fourth row is the hinge of this report第四列為何是本報告的樞紐

Markers on a dot plot say which programmes are detectable; soft-matching says which normal state a cell is closest to, and how closely. Both readings are self-consistent — the activation markers are present and carry 30% of the weight — but the modal counterpart is a resting memory B cell, and 90% of cells agree. That concentration licenses the rest of this report: were the cells spread along a maturation axis, no single normal state would be the right thing to subtract.

dot plot 上的標記所反映的是哪些程式可被偵測；軟性對位所反映的則是細胞最接近哪個正常狀態、以及接近的程度。兩種讀法彼此自洽：活化標記確實存在，並占 30% 的權重，但最主要的對應狀態是靜止記憶 B 細胞，且 90% 的細胞一致指向它。正是這種集中性使本報告的後續分析得以成立；若細胞沿成熟軸線散開，便沒有任何單一正常狀態適合作為相減的對象。

Two corrections, since both propagate. The paper's DOI is 10.1016/j.immuni.2024.01.006; earlier drafts of our repository cited 2024.11.006, which does not resolve. 2Extending the analysis: what is left when development is subtracted延伸分析：扣除分化之後的殘餘

2.1 The subtraction

2.1 相減

Almost every MCL study compares tumour cells with normal B cells in bulk. Because MCL resembles an early B cell, that comparison measures development at least as much as cancer. Section 1 showed it can be done properly: the tumour is pinned to one normal state, so the right control is that state, not the average B cell.

幾乎所有 MCL 研究都是將腫瘤細胞與整體正常 B 細胞相比。由於 MCL 的表型類似早期 B 細胞，這類比較所測得的分化成分至少不少於癌化成分。第 1 節顯示此事可以做得更為正確：腫瘤被釘在單一正常狀態，因此恰當的對照是該狀態，而非平均的 B 細胞。

46,216 tonsil B cells · 6 donors · RNA + ATAC in one nucleus → 46 states, pseudobulk per state per donor
each of 19,975 tumour cells → correlation to every state → softmax over the top 5 → expected normal profile
measured − expected = residual → rebuild the network from RNA + ATAC → compare tumour with normal 46,216 個扁桃體 B 細胞 · 6 位捐贈者 · 同核 RNA + ATAC → 46 個狀態，每狀態每捐贈者 pseudobulk
19,975 個腫瘤細胞逐一 → 對每個狀態計算相關 → 取前 5 名 softmax → 期望的正常表現譜
實測 − 期望 = 殘差 → 以 RNA + ATAC 重建網絡 → 腫瘤與正常對比

The value of the step shows in one gene. After matching, the expected expression of SLC7A11 in the corresponding normal state is 0.001 — effectively off — while the tumour measures 0.206. A window that size is invisible to any comparison that averages over developmental stages, because some normal B-cell states do express the gene.

這一步的價值在單一基因上最為清楚。配對後，SLC7A11 在對應正常狀態的期望表現量為 0.001（等同於不表現），腫瘤實測為 0.206。如此大的窗口在任何將分化階段平均化的比較中都無法顯現，因為部分正常 B 細胞狀態確實表現此基因。

2.2 A seven-letter site, opening genome-wide

2.2 一個七字母位點的全基因組開啟

To ask what drives the residual rather than catalogue it, we rebuilt the regulatory network from both modalities. MCL ATAC was requantified onto the atlas's own 215,204 peak coordinates — the same intervals in tumour and normal, so no peak-calling difference can masquerade as biology — and peak–gene links were computed at metacell level against an empirical null, giving 217,705 links, of which 2,572 are gained and 3,845 lost in the tumour. We then asked which motifs sit under the gained links, against a background matched for both length and GC content.

為追問殘差由什麼驅動，而非僅止於列舉，我們以兩種模態重建調控網絡。MCL 的 ATAC 被重新量化至圖譜自身的 215,204 個 peak 座標上，腫瘤與正常使用完全相同的區間，因此 peak calling 的差異無法偽裝成生物學；再於 metacell 層級對經驗虛無分布計算 peak–基因連結，共 217,705 組，其中腫瘤新生 2,572 條、斷裂 3,845 條。接著我們檢視新生連結之下有哪些 motif，背景同時就長度與 GC 含量配對。

Of 577 motifs tested, 54 are enriched among gained links after correction and 2 depleted. The enriched set is not a proliferation signature: it is one site, written from several directions — TGACTCA, the landing pad of the AP-1 and small-Maf families. FOS::JUN 1.39×, FOS::JUNB 1.38×, FOS::JUND 1.36×, JUN 1.32×, FOSL1::JUN 1.30×, and BACH1 1.38× (padj = 2.3 × 10−4). The other way, B-cell identity factors lose ground: POU2F3 0.70× (padj = 5 × 10−6) and POU5F1 0.83× (4 × 10−4), with seven further POU motifs at 0.75–0.88× that do not survive correction — a trend, not a result.

所檢定的 577 個 motif 中，校正後有 54 個在新生連結富集、2 個耗竭。富集的這一組並非增殖訊號，而是同一個位點被多個家族自不同方向寫入：TGACTCA，即 AP-1 與 small Maf 家族的結合位點。FOS::JUN 1.39×、FOS::JUNB 1.38×、FOS::JUND 1.36×、JUN 1.32×、FOSL1::JUN 1.30×，以及 BACH1 1.38×（padj = 2.3 × 10−4）。反方向上，B 細胞身分因子則流失位點：POU2F3 0.70×（padj = 5 × 10−6）、POU5F1 0.83×（4 × 10−4）；另有七個 POU motif 落在 0.75–0.88× 但未通過校正，僅作為趨勢報告，不列為結果。

2.3 What the site switches on: a connected pathway

2.3 這個位點開啟了什麼：一條相連的路徑

The gene that gains most is SLC7A11, which acquires five regulatory elements absent from normal cells at the matched state (Fig. 4). What makes this more than one gene is that its pathway neighbours come with it, and the pathway is linear: cystine enters through system xc− (SLC7A11 + SLC3A2) → the cell builds glutathione (GCLC, GCLM) → glutathione plus FSP1 (AIFM2) blocks ferroptosis. In DepMap CRISPR screens, MCL lines are selectively dependent on five consecutive members of exactly that line.

增幅最大的是 SLC7A11，它新生出五個在配對正常狀態中不存在的調控元件（圖 4）。使此事不僅涉及單一基因的，是同路徑的鄰近基因一併出現，且該路徑是線性的：胱胺酸經 system xc−（SLC7A11 + SLC3A2）輸入細胞 → 合成穀胱甘肽（GCLC、GCLM）→ 穀胱甘肽與 FSP1（AIFM2）共同阻擋鐵死亡。在 DepMap 的 CRISPR 篩選中，MCL 細胞株正好對這條線上連續五個成員具選擇性依賴。

2.4 Two negative results that change the interpretation

2.4 兩個改變解讀的陰性結果

A rewired axis could be mutation-driven. It is not. In 22 whole-genome-sequenced MCL patients, mutation density in the gained-link peaks is 72.1/Mb against a 65.3/Mb background — 1.10×, no enrichment. Across the 21 axis genes there are zero non-synonymous coding mutations (one synonymous BACH2 variant); eight DepMap MCL lines add two non-LoF NFE2L1 missense variants, 29 cBioPortal cases none. The same pipeline on the same data finds 50 coding mutations in known drivers (ATM 13, SMARCA4 9) at 100% reference-base agreement, and recovers IGH hypermutation in 17 of 22 patients — so the negative is a property of the axis, not the detector.

被重接線的軸線有可能是突變驅動的，但事實並非如此。在 22 位全基因組定序的 MCL 病人中，新生連結 peak 的體細胞突變密度為 72.1/Mb，背景為 65.3/Mb，即 1.10×，無富集。軸線 21 個基因上的非同義編碼突變為 零（僅一個同義 BACH2 變異）；八個 DepMap MCL 細胞株另有兩個非失功能的 NFE2L1 錯義變異，29 例 cBioPortal 病例則為零。同一流程在同一份資料上於已知 driver 檢出 50 個編碼突變（ATM 13、SMARCA4 9），參考鹼基 100% 相符，且 22 位病人中有 17 位重現 IGH 超突變；可見此陰性結果是軸線本身的性質，而非偵測方法的問題。

Therapeutically this is the better of the two possible answers: a purely epigenetic / trans-driven dependency carries no pre-existing stock of resistance mutations.

就治療而言，這是兩種可能答案中較有利的一個：純表觀／trans 驅動的依賴不會事先累積一批抗藥性突變。

2.5 An outside opinion, including the test we had to retract

2.5 外部驗證，包含我們必須撤回的一項檢定

Everything so far comes from two patients, and no third patient-derived MCL multiome exists in public data. The appropriate test is therefore to ask a model that has never seen our samples. We ran three tests: one failed, and cost us an earlier conclusion; two succeeded.

至此的所有結果都出自兩位病人，而公開資料中不存在第三例病人來源的 MCL multiome。因此適當的做法是詢問一個從未見過我們檢體的模型。我們進行三項檢定：一項失敗，並使我們失去先前的一個結論；兩項成功。

The same mistake, three times同一個錯誤出現三次

Three separate artefacts here had one cause: foreground and background groups differing in length or base composition — motif length, then GC content, then peak width. Each produced a clean, large, publishable-looking effect, and each dissolved once the background was matched on the offending variable; the third only after it was public. Effect size tells you nothing about whether a comparison is fair, and a background set is a modelling decision, not a technicality.

本專案三個獨立的假象出自同一個原因：前景組與背景組在長度或鹼基組成上並不相同，依序為 motif 長度、GC 含量與 peak 寬度。每一次都得到乾淨、巨大、看似可以發表的效應，而每一次都在把背景就該變數配對後瓦解；第三次更是在公開之後才被發現。效應大小完全無法說明一項比較是否公平，而背景集的選擇是建模決定，不是技術細節。

2.6 From a DNA site to a protein target

2.6 從 DNA 位點到蛋白質標的

A seven-letter site is not a drug target. The proteins that read it are, but AP-1 and BACH1 family members are mutually redundant — knock out one and another takes over. They share one requirement: at a MARE, the small Maf protein MafG is the obligate partner. MafG is therefore the single point where the whole set converges, and the reason the axis is reachable at all.

七個字母的位點不是藥物標的，讀取它的蛋白質才是；但 AP-1 與 BACH1 家族成員彼此冗餘，敲除其中一個，另一個即可替代。它們共有一項要求：在 MARE 上，small Maf 蛋白 MafG 是必要夥伴。MafG 因此成為整組蛋白唯一收斂的一點，也是這條軸線得以成為可及標的的原因。

Two independent kinds of evidence point at the same residues. A protein language model (ESM-2; 7 proteins, >60,000 variants) measures evolutionary constraint from sequence alone, knowing nothing about structure; the co-crystal 7X5E measures the interface from coordinates, knowing nothing about evolution. For MafG, the median constraint percentile of the 34 interface residues is 17.3 against 59.6 elsewhere (p = 8.3 × 10−11); for FSP1, 26.5 against 56.3 (p = 2.1 × 10−11). Those positions are also poor ground for resistance: the MafG interface tolerates 10.5% of substitutions against 42.4% across the whole protein.

兩種彼此獨立的證據指向同一組殘基。蛋白質語言模型（ESM-2；7 個蛋白質、超過 60,000 個變異）僅自序列測量演化約束，對結構一無所知；共結晶 7X5E 僅自座標測量介面，對演化一無所知。對 MafG 而言，34 個介面殘基的約束度中位百分位為 17.3，其餘位置為 59.6（p = 8.3 × 10−11）；FSP1 則為 26.5 對 56.3（p = 2.1 × 10−11）。這些位置也不利於抗藥性的產生：MafG 介面僅容許 10.5% 的取代，全蛋白質平均為 42.4%。

2.7 Designing against the hub

2.7 針對樞紐進行設計

We generated backbones against the MafG face with RFdiffusion3, designed sequences with ProteinMPNN, and scored every complex by co-folding with Boltz-2, the open model of the AlphaFold 3 class (110 complexes, 159 predictions, five diffusion samples each). The thresholds are not ours: ipSAE > 0.61 comes from 3,766 binders tested at the bench across 15 targets, and ipTM ≥ 0.50, pAE ≤ 10.85 Å, pLDDT ≥ 0.80 are BindCraft defaults, with an independent laboratory converging on the same ~10 Å cut.

我們以 RFdiffusion3 針對 MafG 介面生成骨架，以 ProteinMPNN 設計序列，再以 Boltz-2 共摺疊評分（AlphaFold 3 同級的開源模型；110 個複合體、159 次預測，每個五次 diffusion 取樣）。門檻並非由我們自訂：ipSAE > 0.61 來自 15 個標的共 3,766 個經實驗測試的結合劑，而 ipTM ≥ 0.50、pAE ≤ 10.85 Å、pLDDT ≥ 0.80 是 BindCraft 預設值，另有獨立實驗室收斂到同樣約 10 Å 的切點。

2.9 Limitations

2.9 限制

  • Two patients. Every single-cell claim rests on M102 and M413. Only the cell-state-conditioning test and the DepMap, WGS and literature evidence reach beyond them.
  • 僅有兩位病人。所有單細胞層面的主張均建立在 M102 與 M413 之上。僅細胞狀態條件化檢定與 DepMap、WGS、文獻證據能延伸至這兩位病人之外。
  • The MAFG elements gain coupling but lose openness, and we have no explanation. Our first one came from the width artefact and was retracted; the external test on these elements is null.
  • MAFG 元件獲得協同性卻失去開放度，目前沒有解釋。我們最初的解釋出自寬度假象，已撤回；外部檢定在這些元件上為陰性。
  • Delivery is out of scope by design, and nothing here has been tested at the bench. Every design assumes the protein reaches the nucleus; every claim is a hypothesis with a stated confidence level and, where found, a failure mode.
  • 藥物遞送依設計排除於範圍之外，且本報告沒有任何結果經過實驗驗證。所有設計均預設蛋白質能到達細胞核；每項主張都是帶有明確信心水準的假說，並在我們找到失效模式時一併標明。

3What the two sections add up to兩節綜合說明了什麼

The published analysis used the atlas to show that MCL cells resemble normal B cells. Quantifying that resemblance changes its meaning: the tumour does not drift along a maturation axis — it sits on one state, non-class-switch memory B, more tightly than real memory B cells do. A tumour that is pinned can be subtracted from, and that subtraction turns a reference atlas from a labelling device into a measuring instrument.

已發表的分析以圖譜說明「MCL 細胞類似正常 B 細胞」。將這個「類似」定量之後，其意義便改變了：腫瘤並非沿成熟軸線漂移，而是落在單一狀態（非類別轉換記憶 B 細胞）之上，且其集中程度高於真正的記憶 B 細胞。唯有被釘住的腫瘤才能被相減，而這個相減正是使參考圖譜從標註工具轉為量測儀器的關鍵。

What it reads out is not a known cancer gene but seven letters of DNA, and behind them a metabolic programme that normal B cells at the same position never switch on. The programme has the shape a drug target should have: linear, so no bypass; non-mutated, so no stock of resistance; redundant among the factors that read the site, but convergent on one obligate partner. The design against that partner passes filters calibrated on thousands of real binders — and has never been near a laboratory. The most important number here, that it beats the natural partner, is exactly the one a wet-lab test would be most likely to take away.

它讀出的並非已知癌症基因，而是七個字母的 DNA，以及其背後一套同一位置的正常 B 細胞從不開啟的代謝程式。這套程式具備藥物標的應有的形態：線性，因此沒有旁路；不帶突變，因此沒有既存的抗藥性；讀取位點的因子彼此冗餘，但收斂於唯一一個必要夥伴。針對該夥伴的設計通過了以數千個真實結合劑校準的門檻，卻也從未進入實驗室。本報告最重要的那個數字，即它優於天然夥伴一事，恰好也是濕實驗最可能推翻的一項。
