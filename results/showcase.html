MCL: regulatory rewiring and protein drug design

This study starts from normal B-cell development in the human tonsil. It finds a control circuit that mantle cell lymphoma switches on. It then designs a protein drug against the hub of that circuit.

1. What the method does方法在做什麼

Almost every study asks how MCL differs from a normal B cell. We ask a different question. We ask how MCL differs from the normal B cell at the same stage of development.

我們不問「MCL 和正常 B 細胞差在哪」，而問「MCL 和分化程度相同的那顆正常 B 細胞差在哪」。

STEP 1 Build a reference of normal B-cell development. 46,216 tonsil B cells. 47 states. RNA and chromatin in the same nucleus. STEP 2 Give each tumour cell a soft position on those 47 states. The model then predicts the expression of a normal cell at that position. STEP 3 Subtract the prediction from the measurement. What remains is the part that development cannot explain. STEP 4 Rebuild the control network from RNA and chromatin together. Compare the tumour network with the normal network. STEP 5 Test the result with external models. Then design a drug.

Why step 3 matters. MCL cells look like early B cells. If you compare them with a mixed normal population, you mostly measure development, not cancer. Step 3 removes that part. The signal that remains is small, but it is specific.

2. The circuit that MCL switches onMCL 打開的迴路

The strongest remaining signal is not a known cancer gene. It is a seven-letter piece of DNA: TGACTCA. This sequence is the landing site for two protein families, AP-1 and small Maf. MCL opens many sites that carry it.

最強的殘餘訊號不是已知癌症基因，而是一段 7 個字母的 DNA：TGACTCA。它是 AP-1 與 small Maf 的落腳點。

Read it this way. The pipeline finds 50 mutations in genes that MCL is known to mutate. It finds none in the 21 genes of our pathway. So the pipeline is not broken, and the pathway is not mutated. You cannot treat this circuit by targeting a mutation. You must target the protein that runs it.

同一份資料在已知 driver 抓到 50 個編碼突變，在軸線 21 個基因抓到 0 個。所以不是流程壞了，而是這條路不靠突變打開。

4. External check with AlphaGenome 2用 AlphaGenome 2 做外部檢驗

All results above come from the same two patients. That is a weakness. AlphaGenome 2 is a Google model. It learned from normal tissue only. It gives an independent opinion. We ran three tests. One failed and two succeeded. We report all three.

以上結果全來自同樣兩位病人。AlphaGenome 2 只學過正常組織，可提供獨立意見。三個測試：一個失敗、兩個成功。

Two more tests, with two other outputs of the same model. The link from an element to its gene rested on correlation alone, and the furthest element sits 164 kb away. If a domain boundary stood between them, that correlation would be false. So we asked two new questions: can the elements reach the promoter in three dimensions, and are they locked shut by DNA methylation?

What the four tests mean together

Four tests have now run, with four different outputs of the same model. The balance of evidence between the two arms has changed:

  • SLC7A11 enhancers — now two independent supports. The cell-state test raises all five elements in both patients, and the contact test puts them nearer the promoter than chance allows, which survives correction for width, distance and measured openness. Not supported by the mutation scan, which is expected: a model trained on normal tissue cannot see a link that only cancer makes.
  • MAFG promoter site — now one support, and one retraction. The mutation scan supports it, with a working positive control. The cell-state test does not, which is consistent, because this element is already open in normal cells. The contact test appeared to support it and we retracted that: the signal was peak openness, not position.
  • A mechanism for the SLC7A11 arm. The elements are already wired to the gene in three dimensions, and already unmethylated, yet as closed as peaks that do nothing. The one missing part is the transcription factor. That is why a genome-wide opening of one seven-letter site can land on this gene, and it puts the leverage on the factor or the transporter — not on an epigenetic drug.

Honest limit. The cell-state test feeds the tumour transcriptome to the model. That transcriptome already contains the result of the circuit being on. So the test is independent of our chromatin data, but it is not independent of our hypothesis. The specificity is the part that is hard to explain away: the signal lands on the five elements we named, and not on the other twenty peaks beside them.

證據天平已翻轉：SLC7A11 有兩項獨立外部支持（細胞狀態 + 接觸圖）；MAFG 只剩飽和突變一項，接觸圖那項已撤回。元件「線接好、鎖沒上、門關著」，缺的只有轉錄因子。

5. The target at the protein level蛋白質層級的標的

AP-1 and BACH1 have many members, and the members replace each other. But they all need the same partner at the site: MafG. MafG is the neck of the network. We then asked which parts of MafG a drug should touch.

AP-1 與 BACH1 成員彼此冗餘，但都需要同一個夥伴 MafG。MafG 是這張網的頸口。

6. The drug design藥物設計

We generated protein backbones, wrote sequences onto them, and then folded each design with its target. We then scored the designs with the measure that the field has calibrated against real experiments.

7. What we rejected被我們推翻的假設

A result that only shows what worked is not a result. This table lists every hypothesis that we tested and dropped. Three of them were our own first conclusions.

The pattern worth keeping. Three separate artefacts in this project came from the same cause: a foreground group and a background group that differ in length or in base composition. Motif length, then GC content, then peak width. Each one produced a beautiful and false result.

8. Open problems待解問題

  1. Two patients. No third patient-derived MCL multiome exists in public data. We checked. The cell-state test repeats across both patients, which helps, but two is still two.
  2. MAFG elements gain coupling and lose openness. Part of this is now explained, and part is not. Explained: these elements are CpG-island elements. They read 0.03 for methylation and are open in 7 to 11 percent of normal B cells, so they cannot open much further. They change how they are used, not whether they are on. Not explained: why openness falls instead of staying level. And the coupling itself failed two chromatin tests — co-openness with the promoter does not rise, and element openness does not track MAFG output in the same nucleus. So the coupling at MAFG has RNA evidence only.
  3. The structure model cannot score single mutations on a protein-DNA interface. Published benchmarks agree, and our own data confirms it. The right tools are dedicated energy models. ThermoMPNN ships a matched environment and needs no new download.
  4. The two protein models disagree per mutation. Q82 and Y111 are the clear cases. A third, orthogonal method must break the tie before either position guides a design.
  5. Delivery. The brief set this aside on purpose. A 90-residue protein does not cross a cell membrane by itself. Every design here assumes that it reaches the nucleus.
