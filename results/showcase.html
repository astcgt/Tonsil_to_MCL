MCL: regulatory rewiring and protein drug design

This study starts from normal B-cell development in the human tonsil. It finds a control circuit that mantle cell lymphoma switches on. It then designs a protein drug against the hub of that circuit.

1. What the method does方法在做什麼

Almost every study asks how MCL differs from a normal B cell. We ask a different question. We ask how MCL differs from the normal B cell at the same stage of development.

我們不問「MCL 和正常 B 細胞差在哪」，而問「MCL 和分化程度相同的那顆正常 B 細胞差在哪」。

STEP 1 Build a reference of normal B-cell development. 46,216 tonsil B cells. 47 states. RNA and chromatin in the same nucleus. STEP 2 Give each tumour cell a soft position on those 47 states. The model then predicts the expression of a normal cell at that position. STEP 3 Subtract the prediction from the measurement. What remains is the part that development cannot explain. STEP 4 Rebuild the control network from RNA and chromatin together. Compare the tumour network with the normal network. STEP 5 Test the result with external models. Then design a drug.

Why step 3 matters. MCL cells look like early B cells. If you compare them with a mixed normal population, you mostly measure development, not cancer. Step 3 removes that part. The signal that remains is small, but it is specific.

2. The circuit that MCL switches onMCL 打開的迴路

The strongest remaining signal is not a known cancer gene. It is a seven-letter piece of DNA: TGACTCA. This sequence is the landing site for two protein families, AP-1 and small Maf. MCL opens many sites that carry it.

最強的殘餘訊號不是已知癌症基因，而是一段 7 個字母的 DNA：TGACTCA。它是 AP-1 與 small Maf 的落腳點。

Read it this way. The pipeline finds 50 mutations in genes that MCL is known to mutate. It finds none in the 21 genes of our pathway. So the pipeline is not broken, and the pathway is not mutated. You cannot treat this circuit by targeting a mutation. You must target the protein that runs it.

4. External check with AlphaGenome 2用 AlphaGenome 2 做外部檢驗

All results above come from the same two patients. That is a weakness. AlphaGenome 2 is a Google model. It learned from normal tissue only. It gives an independent opinion. We ran three tests. Two failed and one succeeded. We report all three.

以上結果全來自同樣兩位病人。AlphaGenome 2 只學過正常組織，可提供獨立意見。三個測試：兩個失敗、一個成功。

Test 1 — Failed. A width artefact fooled us. retracted

The question was simple. Does the model predict that the new elements are open in a normal B cell? We compared the new elements with other regions in the same window. The first answer looked excellent. It was wrong.

new elements 新生元件 ordinary control 一般對照 width-matched control 寬度配對對照

Peak width and predicted openness correlate strongly (Spearman +0.74). Our new elements are 3.5 times wider than the controls (1,402 bp against 406 bp). A mean over a wider region is simply larger.
A fixed 501 bp window did not repair the test. Width is a proxy for element strength, not a technical error.
The only valid control is a control of the same width. With that control the effect disappears and reverses: MAFG 0.55× (p = 0.57) and SLC7A11 0.62× (p = 0.37).
We retract the explanation that we first wrote from this test. This is the third time that a length or composition artefact appeared in this project. It is now a standing check: before any foreground-against-background test, match the length and the base composition.

Test 2 — Succeeded for MAFG. confirmed

We changed the question to a causal one. If you break the TGACTCA site, does the model change its prediction of gene expression? We mutated every base in a 39 bp window, one at a time. The other positions in the same window are the internal control, so width cannot matter here. The bars show the effect at the site divided by the effect at the neighbouring positions.

significant 達顯著 positive control 陽性對照 not significant 未達顯著

The positive control works. HMOX1 is a textbook target of this protein family. Its site scores p = 8×10-3 after correction. Better still, only one of three TGACTCA copies near HMOX1 scores. The readout is selective. It does not reward every copy of the motif.
The MAFG promoter site is confirmed. Breaking it changes predicted MAFG expression 3.1 times more than the neighbouring bases do. This holds for two site definitions and two cell types. So MafG controls its own gene through this site. A drug against MafG should weaken the whole loop.
The two distal SLC7A11 elements do not reach significance here. Test 3 answers them.

Test 3 — Succeeded for SLC7A11. confirmed

This test uses our own cells to set the cell state. Condition A is the tumour cells. Condition B is the non-tumour B cells from the same sample. The DNA is identical in both runs. Only the cell state changes. So width, GC content and distance cancel out exactly. The control is the set of peaks in the same window that we did not call rewired.

new elements 新生元件 peaks not called rewired 未重接線 peak

Two patients give almost the same answer. The five SLC7A11 elements open about 2× under the tumour state. The other peaks in the same window open about 1.2×. M413: p = 7.9×10-4. M102: p = 1.9×10-5. The 19 MAFG elements show nothing (p = 0.67 and 0.95).
The important detail: we give the model only the transcriptome. Nothing in that input tells the model which peaks we called rewired. The model still puts the signal on exactly those five.

The encoder separates the two cell states

The documentation asks for this check before you trust the conditioning. We classify single cells from their embedding. The bars show the area under the ROC curve. A value of 0.5 means no information. A value of 1.0 means perfect separation.

The naive ratio of distances suggested poor separation (0.61). That ratio was misleading. The clouds overlap, but their centres are far apart. A permutation test gives p = 5×10-4, which is the floor for 2,000 permutations. The conditioning carries real information.

Two more tests, with two other outputs of the same model. The link from an element to its gene rested on correlation alone, and the furthest element sits 164 kb away. If a domain boundary stood between them, that correlation would be false. So we asked two new questions: can the elements reach the promoter in three dimensions, and are they locked shut by DNA methylation?

What the four tests mean together

Four tests have now run, with four different outputs of the same model. The balance of evidence between the two arms has changed:

  • SLC7A11 enhancers — now two independent supports. The cell-state test raises all five elements in both patients, and the contact test puts them nearer the promoter than chance allows, which survives correction for width, distance and measured openness. Not supported by the mutation scan, which is expected: a model trained on normal tissue cannot see a link that only cancer makes.
  • MAFG promoter site — now one support, and one retraction. The mutation scan supports it, with a working positive control. The cell-state test does not, which is consistent, because this element is already open in normal cells. The contact test appeared to support it and we retracted that: the signal was peak openness, not position.
  • A mechanism for the SLC7A11 arm. The elements are already wired to the gene in three dimensions, and already unmethylated, yet as closed as peaks that do nothing. The one missing part is the transcription factor. That is why a genome-wide opening of one seven-letter site can land on this gene, and it puts the leverage on the factor or the transporter — not on an epigenetic drug.

Honest limit. The cell-state test feeds the tumour transcriptome to the model. That transcriptome already contains the result of the circuit being on. So the test is independent of our chromatin data, but it is not independent of our hypothesis. The specificity is the part that is hard to explain away: the signal lands on the five elements we named, and not on the other twenty peaks beside them.

證據天平已翻轉：SLC7A11 有兩項獨立外部支持（細胞狀態 + 接觸圖）；MAFG 只剩飽和突變一項，接觸圖那項已撤回。元件「線接好、鎖沒上、門關著」，缺的只有轉錄因子。

5. The target at the protein level蛋白質層級的標的

AP-1 and BACH1 have many members, and the members replace each other. But they all need the same partner at the site: MafG. MafG is the neck of the network. We then asked which parts of MafG a drug should touch.

AP-1 與 BACH1 成員彼此冗餘，但都需要同一個夥伴 MafG。MafG 是這張網的頸口。

Figure 8. Two independent sources point at the same residues

A protein language model gives each position a constraint score. A low number means that evolution does not allow change there. The whole-protein baseline is 50. The crystal structure tells us which positions sit in the interface. The two lists agree.

interface residues 介面殘基 all other residues 其餘殘基

MafG: p = 8.3×10-11. FSP1: p = 2.1×10-11. Sequence evolution and atomic coordinates are completely different kinds of evidence. They select the same residues.
Scope limit. The agreement holds for which positions matter. It does not hold for how bad a given substitution is. We measured that directly: the two readouts do not correlate per mutation (Spearman −0.10, p = 0.71).

Figure 9. The tumour has little room to escape

The bars show the share of substitutions that the model accepts at the interface, next to the share for the whole protein. A low share means that the tumour cannot easily mutate away from the drug.

interface 介面 whole protein 全蛋白質

The MafG interface accepts 10.5% of substitutions. The whole protein accepts 42.4%. That is one quarter of the room. Escape mutations concentrate at four positions only: A94, Q82, M100 and K101.
One conflict to flag. The language model calls Q82 free to change. The structure model says that Q82A damages the interface by 6.4 standard deviations. Do not drop or keep such a position on one model alone.

Figure 10. Point mutations at the dimer interface

The bars show how much each mutation damages the interface, in units of the noise of the method. We measured the noise from five repeat runs of the unchanged complex. The control mutations sit on a surface that the partner never touches.

interface mutation 介面突變 unrelated surface 無關表面對照

Four zipper residues stand far above the noise: L79A at 22σ, K83A at 15σ, N97A at 14σ and L93A at 12σ. The three controls stay below 1.5σ. As a group, 9 of 11 interface mutations pass 3σ, and 0 of 3 controls do (p = 0.030).
Where this method fails. The same approach on the protein-DNA interface returns nothing at all. All 24 mutations stay inside the noise, including charge reversals that should be lethal. The baseline score is saturated at 0.965. That readout has zero information, not weak information.

6. The drug design藥物設計

We generated protein backbones, wrote sequences onto them, and then folded each design with its target. We then scored the designs with the measure that the field has calibrated against real experiments.

生成骨架 → 設計序列 → 與標的共摺疊 → 用領域校準過的指標評分。

Figure 11. Scored against wet-lab calibrated thresholds

The bars show ipSAE, an interface-focused score. Three independent studies find that it separates real binders from failures better than the common score does. The published threshold is 0.61. It comes from 3,766 binders that were tested in the laboratory. The dashed line marks that threshold.

Two designs pass all four filters. The best design scores 0.631. The natural partner, NRF2, scores 0.539 and passes only three of four. So the designs beat the natural partner on the measure that the field recommends, not only on the common one.
This score also separates much better than the common one on our own data. Designs against scrambled controls: p = 2.2×10-5 with ipSAE, against p = 3.5×10-4 with ipTM. The range is 48-fold with ipSAE and 2-fold with ipTM.
Interface error is the cleanest single filter. Passing designs sit at 6.9 to 8.5 Å. The controls sit at 17.4 to 19.6 Å. There is no overlap. Two independent laboratories set this threshold near 10 Å from separate data.

Figure 13. The reason is solvent access

The bars show how exposed the target residues are. A mini-protein can only touch an exposed surface.

A design rule that the failure taught us. Evolutionary constraint plus a structural interface finds the functional core. Whether a protein drug can reach that core is an independent third condition.
A buried pocket belongs to a small molecule. An exposed interface belongs to a mini-protein. FSP1 is still a good target. It needs a different kind of drug.

Figure 14. Added sequence: turn a blocker into a shredder

We attached a degradation tag to the binder. The tumour cell then destroys MafG instead of only blocking it. Both interfaces must score well. The bars show each interface.

Put the tag at the N-terminus, use the ETGE tag from KEAP1, and keep the linker at 9 to 18 residues. The VHL tag fails everywhere (0.11 to 0.14). It needs a chemical modification that the model correctly does not add.
A useful coincidence. MCL runs the NRF2 pathway hard, so the cell already holds a large amount of KEAP1. The machine that the tumour builds to protect itself is the machine that removes its own hub.
Still open. The fusion scores 0.38 to 0.42 against KEAP1, while the bare ETGE peptide scores 0.957. The linker needs more work.

7. What we rejected被我們推翻的假設

A result that only shows what worked is not a result. This table lists every hypothesis that we tested and dropped. Three of them were our own first conclusions.

The pattern worth keeping. Three separate artefacts in this project came from the same cause: a foreground group and a background group that differ in length or in base composition. Motif length, then GC content, then peak width. Each one produced a beautiful and false result.

8. Open problems待解問題

  1. Two patients. No third patient-derived MCL multiome exists in public data. We checked. The cell-state test repeats across both patients, which helps, but two is still two.
  2. MAFG elements gain coupling and lose openness. Part of this is now explained, and part is not. Explained: these elements are CpG-island elements. They read 0.03 for methylation and are open in 7 to 11 percent of normal B cells, so they cannot open much further. They change how they are used, not whether they are on. Not explained: why openness falls instead of staying level. And the coupling itself failed two chromatin tests — co-openness with the promoter does not rise, and element openness does not track MAFG output in the same nucleus. So the coupling at MAFG has RNA evidence only.
  3. The structure model cannot score single mutations on a protein-DNA interface. Published benchmarks agree, and our own data confirms it. The right tools are dedicated energy models. ThermoMPNN ships a matched environment and needs no new download.
  4. The two protein models disagree per mutation. Q82 and Y111 are the clear cases. A third, orthogonal method must break the tie before either position guides a design.
  5. Delivery. The brief set this aside on purpose. A 90-residue protein does not cross a cell membrane by itself. Every design here assumes that it reaches the nucleus.
